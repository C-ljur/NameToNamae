Namaé replaces RimWorld's built-in English pawn and animal names when they are generated.

The included Japanese name pack contains kana readings for 27,914 pawn names and 1,962 animal names. Separate name-pack mods can add other languages.

[b]Pawns[/b]
First names, last names, and nicknames are replaced on generation. Existing names are left alone unless you use the one-way conversion in the mod settings. Back up the save before converting them.

[b]Animals[/b]
Colony animals can receive proper names when they are born, hatched, tamed, self-tamed, or join the colony. Player-entered names and existing proper names are left alone. Automatic naming and duplicate avoidance have separate settings.

[b]Also included[/b]
• Japanese text for developer-mode actions
• Reports for new and untranslated names
• Experimental line breaking for Japanese, Chinese, and Korean (off by default)

[b]Requires[/b]
• RimWorld 1.6
• [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2009463077]Harmony[/url]

Name-pack mods must depend on Namaé and load after it.
